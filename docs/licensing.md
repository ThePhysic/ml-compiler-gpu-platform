# License decision — pending owner approval

No root license or owner-approved licensing terms were found in the reviewed
repository or its license-file history. This page compares options; it grants no
license and changes no repository/package metadata. A public GitHub repository
does not itself supply a general software reuse license. GitHub separately permits
viewing and forking public repositories under its terms.
([GitHub licensing guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository))

| Consideration | MIT | Apache-2.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reuse | Broad permissions to use, modify, redistribute and sell software | Broad copyright permissions to reproduce, modify and distribute the work |
| Redistribution notices | Retain the copyright and permission notice in copies or substantial portions | Supply the license, retain applicable notices, identify modified files, and preserve required NOTICE attribution when present |
| Patent terms | No separate express patent-license clause in the text | Express contributor patent grant, with termination conditions for specified patent litigation |
| Warranty | Disclaims warranties and liability | Disclaims warranties and limits liability, subject to stated qualifications |

The comparison is based on the complete [MIT text published by OSI](https://opensource.org/license/mit)
and the [Apache-2.0 terms published by the Apache Software Foundation](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

For this compact engineering portfolio, **MIT is the suggested option for owner
consideration** because its notice requirements are short and easy to carry with
reused examples. Choose Apache-2.0 instead if explicit contributor patent terms
are a priority and its additional notice obligations fit the intended collaboration.
Neither recommendation selects terms on the owner's behalf.

The outstanding choice is the owner's exact license authorization. Before a
`LICENSE` file is added, confirm the owner's right to license the relevant work,
the copyright holder and year, then use the complete approved official text and
make metadata consistent. Until then, status remains **pending owner decision**.
